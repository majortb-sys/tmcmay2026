Strategic Interpretation
The market is moving in two connected lanes — mature campaign execution and strategic CDP/data unification — and the winning opportunity sits at their intersection.
Lane 1 · Mature & Execution-Heavy
Marketing Cloud, campaign operations, journeys, and B2B marketing automation. Well-established demand with Salesforce as the dominant incumbent.
Lane 2 · Strategic & Foundational
CDP, Data Cloud, unified profiles, data integration, and customer data activation. Clients want data unified to power journeys, personalization, loyalty, and analytics.
